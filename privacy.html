Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide: name, email, phone, profile details, portfolio, payment records, messages, and job data. We also collect usage data such as pages visited and device information.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used to operate the marketplace, process payments, facilitate communication, send notifications, improve our services, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We share necessary information between clients and freelancers for job completion. We do not sell personal data. Payment processors (Khalti, eSewa) receive transaction data as required.

4. Data Security

Passwords are hashed. Data is stored securely with access controls. We use encryption for sensitive transactions. No system is 100% secure; report concerns immediately.

5. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your profile data via Settings. You can control notification preferences and profile visibility. Request data export or deletion by contacting privacy@freelancefactory.com.

6. GDPR Compliance

For EU users, we comply with GDPR principles: lawful processing, data minimization, purpose limitation, and the right to erasure. Our legal basis is contract performance and legitimate interest.

7. Cookies

We use session storage and local storage for authentication and preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

8. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Transaction records are kept for 7 years for legal compliance. Deactivated accounts are anonymized after 90 days.

9. Children's Privacy

FreelanceFactory is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Contact

Privacy concerns: privacy@freelancefactory.com
